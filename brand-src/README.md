# VEERLY brand kit

> **Palette note (v5):** these are Mo's original brand-kit source files and still use the old violet. The live site no longer uses them directly: its colour is Lemon Squeezy true blue `#0075AD` (see `DESIGN.md`), and the served logos and icons in `public/` and `app/` were recoloured to match.

## Logo (logo/)
- **veerly-logo**: the V mark plus the name. Use it on white or light backgrounds.
- **veerly-logo-white**: for violet or dark backgrounds. The *-on-violet.png files show it in place.
- **veerly-logo-black**: one colour, for print, stamps and documents.
- **veerly-mark**: the V on its own, for small spaces and avatars.
- **veerly-wordmark**: the name on its own.

Every logo comes as SVG (sharp at any size, best for print and the web) and as PNG at 512, 1024 and 2048 px
wide, with a transparent background.

**Rules:**
- Keep clear space around the logo at least the height of the yellow flag.
- Don't stretch, rotate or recolour it.
- Never put the violet logo on a violet background; use the white one there.

## App icon (icon/)
The violet rounded square with the white V, at 32 to 1024 px, plus the 180 px Apple touch icon. Use it for
social profile pictures (512 or 1024).

## Mascot (mascot/)
Dot, VEERLY's guide from the story lessons.

## Colours (colours/)
- **VEERLY violet** `#6A45FF`: Buttons, links, the mark
- **Stage violet** `#4A1FD6`: Big violet backgrounds, app icon
- **Proof yellow** `#FFD84D`: The flag on the V, highlights
- **Ink** `#17122E`: Text, black logo
- **Lilac** `#F1EDFF`: Soft violet backgrounds
- **Proof green** `#08714F`: Verified work, progress
- **Coral** `#FF6B4A`: New notifications only
- **Ground** `#FAFAFC`: Page background

## Fonts
- **Bricolage Grotesque** (ExtraBold 800): the logo and headlines. https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Bricolage+Grotesque
- **Geist**: body text on the website. https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Geist
