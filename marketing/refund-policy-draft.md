# Veerly refund policy (draft for Mo to approve)

Status: draft. Not linked from the navigation or the FAQ until Mo approves it. The same text is on the Terms page, marked as a draft.

## The short version

You can ask for a full refund up to 7 days before your cohort starts, as long as you have not opened or duplicated the workspace. After that, or once you have accessed the workspace, we do not offer a refund, except where the law requires it.

## The details

- The founding cohort is a one-time payment (₦10,000 in Nigeria, or $7.99 where US-dollar checkout is available). There is no subscription.
- To request a refund, email us using the contact details on the Terms page, from the same email address you paid with, and include your payment reference.
- We will confirm the request and, if it qualifies, refund the amount you paid to the original payment method. Banks can take a few working days to show the money.
- If Veerly has to cancel a cohort, or cannot deliver the workspace you paid for, you will be offered a full refund or a place on a later cohort.
- This draft is not legal advice and may change before enrolment opens on 1 January 2027.
